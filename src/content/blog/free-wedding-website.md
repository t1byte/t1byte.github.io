A wedding website is your digital hub for everything guests need to know:

- Event details (date, time, venue)
- RSVP management
- Travel & accommodation info

- Photo gallery
- Love story & wedding party bios

## How to Set It Up in 5 Minutes

### Step 1: Get the Template

Download the template from our website.

### Step 2: Customize

Edit `index.html` to change:
- Names and dates
- Photos (replace images in `assets/images/`)
- Background music (replace files in `assets/mp3/`)
- Colors and fonts

### Step 3: Deploy

Upload to any static host:
- **Cloudflare Pages** or **Netlify** (free tier)
- **Vercel** (free tier)
- **Hostinger** or any shared hosting

### Step 4: Share

Send your guests the link — done!
