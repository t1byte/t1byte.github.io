Planning a wedding is one of the most exciting — and overwhelming — things you'll ever do. Whether you have a full year or just a few months, this **month-by-month wedding planning checklist** will keep you organized and stress-free.

> **Pro Tip:** Create your own [free wedding website](/demo/) to share details with guests, manage RSVPs, and collect well-wishes.

## 12 Months Before the Wedding

- **Set a budget.** Decide who's contributing and allocate funds for venue, catering, attire, photography, flowers, and entertainment.
- **Choose a date.** Check holidays, peak seasons, and venue availability.
- **Draft your guest list.** Estimate headcount before touring venues.
- **Create a [free wedding website](/demo/).** Share your love story, event details, registry, and RSVP form in one beautiful page.
- **Hire a wedding planner.** If your budget allows, a planner saves months of stress.

## 10–11 Months Before

- **Book your venue.** Popular spots fill 9–12 months ahead.
- **Research and book vendors:** photographer, caterer, florist, DJ/band.
- **Start dress shopping.** Custom dresses take 6–8 months to make and alter.
- **Choose your wedding party.** Ask bridesmaids and groomsmen early.

## 8–9 Months Before

- **Send save-the-dates.** Give guests time to book travel.
- **Book accommodations.** Reserve hotel blocks for out-of-town guests.
- **Schedule tastings.** Menu tasting with your caterer or venue.
- **Plan your registry.** Register at 2–3 stores with varied price points.

> **Save on Photography:** Consider using a [professional photo booth rental](https://amzn.to/4fV2R3X) for candid guest photos — it doubles as entertainment and keepsake.

## 6–7 Months Before

- **Order wedding attire.** Dress, suit, shoes, accessories.
- **Book hair & makeup trial.** Schedule trials with your stylist.
- **Plan honeymoon.** Book flights and hotels early for best rates.
- **Finalize guest list.** Get addresses and dietary restrictions.

## 4–5 Months Before

- **Send formal invitations.** 6–8 weeks before is standard; send earlier for destination weddings.
- **Order wedding rings.** Allow time for sizing and engraving.
- **Arrange transportation.** Book vehicles for wedding party and guests.
- **Plan ceremony details.** Write vows, choose readings, pick music.

## 2–3 Months Before

- **Finalize seating chart.** Use a seating chart tool to manage table assignments.
- **Track RSVPs.** Follow up with guests who haven't responded.
- **Purchase wedding favors.** Order personalized gifts for guests.
- **Get marriage license.** Check local requirements — some have waiting periods.

> **Set the Mood:** A [portable Bluetooth speaker](https://amzn.to/3ZYXabc) for the getting-ready room or outdoor ceremony can make all the difference.

## 1 Month Before

- **Confirm all vendors.** Finalize timelines and payments.
- **Submit final headcount** to your caterer/venue.
- **Attend final dress fitting.** Bring your shoes and undergarments.
- **Create day-of timeline.** Share with vendors and wedding party.
- **Pack your wedding day emergency kit.** Safety pins, stain remover, snacks, water.

## 1 Week Before

- **Confirm RSVPs.** Chase down any remaining responses.
- **Print ceremony programs, menus, place cards.**
- **Update your [wedding website](/demo/)** with final timeline and live-stream links.
- **Delegate.** Assign a point person for vendors, another for guest questions.
- **Rest, hydrate, and enjoy.** The hard part is done!

## Wedding Day

- **Eat breakfast.** You'll need the energy.
- **Assign someone to collect gifts and cards.**
- **Breathe.** Soak in every moment — it goes by fast.
